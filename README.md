# Mjakulufu Family Management System

## 📌 About The Project

**Mjakulufu Family Management System** is a digital platform designed to help manage and organize family activities, information, and resources in a secure and efficient way.

The system provides a centralized place where family members can manage family records, track contributions, monitor expenses, organize meetings, share announcements, and generate reports.

The main goal of this system is to strengthen family communication, transparency, accountability, and proper management of family resources.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* To maintain accurate records of family members
* To manage family contributions digitally
* To track family expenses and financial activities
* To organize family meetings and events
* To provide announcements and important family information
* To improve transparency in family financial management
* To provide reports for better decision making

---
